Four ways the app can be organised. Each one treats a different question as the main one. The sample household and its numbers are the same in all four; directions A and B show Wednesday 7 October 2026, and C and D show the finished month of September 2026.

A

Today list

The app opens on the people expected today. Marking someone takes one tap, and the list shrinks as the day goes on.

  • Only today's schedule is shown; people not due today are folded away.
  • Deliveries ask for a quantity, with the usual amount already filled in.
  • A reminder at the expected time and a home-screen widget would both land on this list.
  • Money sits on a separate tab and stays out of the daily routine.

B

Register page

The app opens on a ruled page like the paper khata: one row per person, one column per day. Tapping a cell marks it.

  • The whole household's week is visible at once, so a missed day stands out.
  • Blue tick for came, red cross for absent, a number for litres, a dash for days off.
  • Past days can be corrected in place; the page scrolls sideways through the month.
  • Each person has a full-month page with counts against their target days.

One person's month

C

Month accounts

The app opens on what the household owes. Attendance is still recorded, but it is shown as the reason for each amount.

  • Each person has a rate: a fixed monthly fee, a fee per visit, or a price per unit.
  • The amount for the month is worked out from the marked days and litres.
  • Advances and part payments are subtracted, and each bill is marked paid when settled.
  • A statement can be sent to the person, for example on WhatsApp, so both sides see the same count.

One person's statement

D

Combined month

One screen shows the whole household for the whole month. Every person has a colour, and each day carries a mark for everyone who was due.

  • Calendar form: a filled dot means came, a red ring means absent, no dot means not due that day.
  • Tapping a day lists who came, who was absent and how much was delivered.
  • Strip form: one row of 30 days per person, so each person's gaps and the month count are visible together.
  • Tapping a name in the legend would filter either form down to that person.

This would be the "Month" tab of direction A, with a switch between the two forms. The sample month is September 2026, which matches the amounts in direction C.
